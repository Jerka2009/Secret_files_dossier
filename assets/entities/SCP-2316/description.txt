SCP-2316 is a cognitohazardous phenomenon associated with a lake containing a group of human bodies floating together near the surface. The anomaly attacks cognition as much as it attacks the physical senses: observers may experience false familiarity, altered recognition, and a dangerous compulsion to approach. The Foundation restricts direct observation and relies on remote equipment and specially screened personnel. The bodies do not behave like ordinary independent corpses and may function as parts of a collective anomalous system. The central survival problem is epistemic: exposure can change what the victim thinks they recognize, making memory and identification unreliable during an encounter.
