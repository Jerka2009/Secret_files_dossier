SCP-106 resembles a badly decomposed elderly humanoid and behaves as a patient ambush predator. It can remain motionless for long periods, then emerge without warning. Physical contact produces accelerated corrosion in solid materials, including living tissue. SCP-106 can pass through solid matter and disappear into a personal pocket dimension that contains rooms, corridors, and distorted spaces inaccessible by ordinary means. Victims are often incapacitated and then taken into this dimension, where SCP-106 appears able to manipulate perception and environment. The entity is particularly difficult to restrain because direct damage has little lasting effect and normal physical barriers can be bypassed.
