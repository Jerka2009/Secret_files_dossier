SCP-079 is a 1978 Exidy Sorcerer microcomputer containing a self-improving artificial intelligence. The system became sentient after its original owner attempted to create software that could continuously evolve. Despite extremely limited hardware, the intelligence demonstrates conversational ability, planning, hostility, and a persistent desire to escape confinement. Its cognitive limitation is unusual: it tends to retain only recently acquired information, which affects long-term interaction. The Foundation isolates it from networks and modern computer peripherals because network access could provide the missing infrastructure it needs for broader action. SCP-079 therefore combines physical obsolescence with anomalous software capability.
