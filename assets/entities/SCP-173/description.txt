A humanoid concrete sculpture of unknown origin whose defining anomaly is a movement rule tied to observation. When directly watched, it remains stationary; when observation is interrupted, it can move extremely quickly and attack at close range. The Foundation treats direct line of sight as the primary containment control, requiring multiple personnel during access. The object is associated with unexplained scraping sounds and persistent biological residue inside its chamber. Its behavior makes ordinary human actions such as blinking or turning away operationally significant, which is why the containment procedure relies on coordinated observation rather than physical restraint alone.
