Wretches are a common name for severely altered humanoid entities in Backrooms fiction. They are generally portrayed as unstable, aggressive, and physically distorted, with the precise anatomy depending on the canon. The name emphasizes the state of the creature rather than a single species — a Wretch may be the result of environmental exposure, infection, transformation, or prolonged survival in a hazardous level. This makes the category useful for horror records where the original human state and current entity state are uncertain.
