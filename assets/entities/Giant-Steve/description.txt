Giant Steve is a later community legend modeled on Giant Alex but presented as more aggressive. The classic entry describes a twelve-block-tall Steve-like entity associated with acacia, swamp, and oak biomes and a specific community seed. It leaves larger footprints than Giant Alex and is said to attack villages rather than merely observe from the distance. The story also escalates the threat into game interference, including crashes and account-related effects, although those are purely part of the creepypasta fiction. As with other Minecraft legends, multiple versions exist and are not mutually consistent.
