SCP-3008-2 instances are humanoid entities inhabiting the extradimensional interior of SCP-3008. Although outwardly similar to human retail employees, they have inconsistent and exaggerated proportions and no facial features. During the anomalous “night” cycle they become aggressive and attack other lifeforms, often while repeating variations of the store’s closing announcement. During the “day” cycle they revert to passive wandering behavior. They are part of a wider survival ecosystem in which trapped humans build settlements and defensive structures. Their danger is therefore contextual: a normally passive employee-like entity becomes a coordinated predator when the store lights go out.
