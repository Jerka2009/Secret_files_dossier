SCP-1000 is a nocturnal, omnivorous ape species that occupies the same broad hominid family as humans and other great apes. Adult individuals vary from approximately 1.5 to 3 m in height and possess strong visual acuity, large eyes, and high intelligence. The SCP narrative expands the familiar “Bigfoot” idea into the remains of a once-global civilization that existed alongside early humanity. A hereditary anomaly associated with SCP-1000 can kill susceptible hominids who observe an affected individual, making surveillance itself hazardous. The Foundation therefore treats sightings as evidence requiring investigation, concealment, and population monitoring.
