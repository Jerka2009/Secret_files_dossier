SCP-1471 begins as a seemingly ordinary mobile application distributed without a listed developer. After installation, the affected device begins receiving anomalous images featuring a tall, dark, canine-like humanoid figure. The subject appears progressively closer to the affected user as exposure continues, eventually manifesting in the user’s immediate environment or perception. Because the phenomenon spreads through digital distribution, the Foundation treats the application itself as a vector and attempts to seize affected devices and remove the software from circulation. The horror of SCP-1471 is cumulative: the user is first shown a distant figure, then an increasingly intimate presence that becomes difficult to distinguish from ordinary surroundings.
