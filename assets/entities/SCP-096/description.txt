SCP-096 is a very tall, extremely thin humanoid with disproportionately long arms and a nearly depigmented body. In its passive state it is withdrawn and often appears distressed. The critical hazard is visual exposure: learning its facial image, even indirectly through an image, can trigger a violent pursuit response. Once triggered, SCP-096 becomes exceptionally fast and focused on the person who viewed its face, ignoring most other concerns until that target is reached. After the attack it returns to a depressed or passive state. Containment therefore prioritizes eliminating visual exposure and avoiding ordinary optical surveillance inside the cell.
