Smilers are among the most recognizable Backrooms entities. They are typically perceived as a pair of reflective eyes and a wide, unnatural grin floating in darkness, with the rest of the body obscured or absent from view. Smilers prefer unlit environments and can become dangerous when a wanderer attracts their attention with light or movement. Different canons disagree on exact anatomy and behavior, but the stable visual rule is simple: the smile is visible before the creature is. This makes them especially effective as ambush predators in blackouts, tunnels, and unlit hallways.
