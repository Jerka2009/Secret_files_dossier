The Thing on Level 7 is a deliberately vague entity concept associated with a dangerous aquatic level. Most descriptions rely on absence rather than anatomy: something is present below, around, or beyond visibility, and the wanderer cannot reliably determine its size or location. The horror comes from entering an environment where open water destroys the usual assumption that danger must be visible. This entity is best treated as an unknown aquatic predator rather than a creature with a fixed silhouette.
