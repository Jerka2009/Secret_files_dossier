Null is a dark, player-shaped Minecraft creepypasta entity commonly depicted as a black silhouette. Stories use Null as a more abstract counterpoint to Herobrine: rather than being a strange version of Steve, Null is framed as a manifestation of absence, corruption, or a hidden threat. Common signs include a silent black figure at a distance, unexplained messages, missing or altered game elements, and sightings that end when the player tries to approach. Some stories connect Null directly to Herobrine, but that relationship is fan-made and changes from one continuity to another.
