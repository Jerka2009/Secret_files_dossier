SCP-2006 is a small anomalous spherical entity whose primary objective is to frighten as many people as possible. Its anomalous biology allows it to change shape, mass, density, chemistry, and voice into almost any form it chooses. It is therefore less dangerous because of its default appearance than because it can become a threat tailored to the fears of its audience. The Foundation manipulates its media diet by supplying intentionally low-quality horror films so that the entity continues to imitate familiar fictional monsters rather than discovering more consequential sources of human fear. This containment strategy treats knowledge and preference as part of the anomaly itself.
