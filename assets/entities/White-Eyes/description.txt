White Eyes is an early Minecraft horror motif centered on two bright white pixels appearing in dark underground environments. The story links the eyes to ambient cave noises and the psychological feeling that something is observing the player from outside the visible world. Unlike later character-style creepypastas, White Eyes is deliberately minimal: the main horror is that almost nothing is visible except the eyes. Later community interpretations sometimes turned the motif into a full creature, but the earliest version is closer to an unseen observer or paranormal presence.
