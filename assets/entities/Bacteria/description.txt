Bacteria is a name used for a foundational class of Backrooms creature in several fan canons. Unlike the humanoid entities, it is often conceptualized as a large biological or environmental organism whose presence changes the surrounding level. Different versions describe it as a creature moving through walls, a colony-like mass, or a source of contamination. Because the concept is less standardized than Smilers or Hounds, the most accurate database treatment is to mark the appearance and height as variable while retaining its role as an environmental biological threat.
