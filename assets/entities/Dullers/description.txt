Dullers are dark, humanoid entities with reduced facial and sensory features. Different Backrooms canons give them different levels of aggression, but many versions describe them as capable of interacting with walls in ways that ordinary humans cannot. This can turn corridors into hunting spaces: a Duller may appear to occupy one side of a wall while attacking from the other. Their body design is intentionally plain and difficult to interpret, making a distant encounter ambiguous until the entity begins moving through architecture.
