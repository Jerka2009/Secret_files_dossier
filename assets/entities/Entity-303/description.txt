Entity 303 is a Minecraft creepypasta character often presented as a successor or rival to Herobrine. The classic version describes a white-hooded humanoid with bloodshot eyes that can appear in worlds or server logs and is associated with anomalous crashes, messages, and stalking. Its alleged abilities vary by retelling, but recurring powers include teleportation, invisibility, world alteration, and deliberate interference with game systems. The legend has accumulated a large amount of later fan-created material, so the safest way to treat it is as a family of related stories rather than a single fixed canon.
