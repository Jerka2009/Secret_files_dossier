Herobrine is the best-known Minecraft creepypasta figure: a visually familiar player avatar whose eyes are described as blank or pupil-less. Community stories usually portray him as an unseen or semi-visible observer who alters terrain, watches the player from a distance, or appears briefly before vanishing. Repeated motifs include unnatural tunnels, small structures in otherwise ordinary terrain, damaged trees, and sightings at the edge of fog or render distance. The legend has changed across retellings, so individual powers vary widely; the stable core is the idea of an impossible second player existing in a supposedly single-player world.
