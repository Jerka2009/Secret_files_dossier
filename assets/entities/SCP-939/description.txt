SCP-939 is a species of predatory, warm-blooded anomalous organisms that hunt through a combination of stealth, speed, and vocal mimicry. They can reproduce sounds associated with human speech and use this ability to lure prey toward hidden positions. Their bodies are adapted for close-range pursuit, and their lack of conventional eyes does not prevent effective hunting. The most dangerous encounters occur when a victim responds to a familiar voice or assumes a distant call is coming from another person. Foundation containment focuses on controlling individual specimens and preventing their acoustic deception from creating false assumptions about the location or identity of nearby humans.
