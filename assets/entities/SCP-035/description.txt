SCP-035 is an anomalous theatrical mask capable of exerting strong psychological influence over nearby humans. When worn by a compatible host, it can replace or suppress the host personality and use the body as a physical vessel. The mask also produces a corrosive liquid that damages surrounding materials and can create hazardous conditions in its containment area. SCP-035 is highly manipulative in conversation, using persuasion, flattery, threats, and strategic information to influence researchers. Its danger therefore comes not only from physical effects but from its ability to turn a person into an unwilling agent.
