SCP-049 is a humanoid entity dressed like a traditional European plague doctor. The entity is intelligent, articulate, and capable of discussing medicine, disease, and its own unusual understanding of illness. Physical contact with exposed skin can produce rapid death through an anomalous biological effect. SCP-049 also performs surgery on corpses in an attempt to create reanimated subjects it considers cured. The Foundation contains it as a cooperative but dangerous humanoid: it can communicate and sometimes assist with medical tasks, yet its interpretation of disease is radically different from modern medicine and can lead it to kill subjects it considers infected.
