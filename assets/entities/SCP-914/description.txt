SCP-914 is a large clockwork machine that transforms objects placed into one booth and retrieved from another after the operator selects a setting from Rough through Very Fine. The machine contains millions of moving parts yet has no obvious conventional power source beyond an internal mainspring. Results are often related to the input but can become increasingly strange or hazardous as the setting becomes finer. Biological testing is heavily restricted because some transformations have produced severe injuries and unpredictable outcomes. The anomaly is less about direct aggression than about uncontrolled transformation: even familiar objects can emerge altered in material, function, identity, or physical state.
