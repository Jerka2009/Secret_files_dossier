Deathmoths are colossal moth-like entities with irregular wings and predatory mouthparts. They resemble familiar moths only in broad outline; their scale and behavior are what make them dangerous. Community canon commonly places them in large numbers across multiple levels, with especially notable encounters in damp or abandoned areas. Some variants are more hostile than others, creating a sliding threat rather than one uniform species behavior. Their size makes ordinary moth imagery uncanny: a familiar insect becomes a room-sized or human-sized predator that can occupy ceilings, corridors, or open spaces.
