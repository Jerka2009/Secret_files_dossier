Clumps are masses of intertwined human-like limbs that live in cramped architectural spaces such as vents, cabinets, drains, pipes, and other narrow passages. Their bodies are difficult to read as a single organism, which is part of their threat: a wanderer can mistake an appendage-filled space for structural debris until the creature moves. Clumps are known for ambush behavior and can exploit the confined geometry of their habitat better than a human can. The danger increases when explorers inspect small maintenance areas or attempt to crawl through tight shortcuts.
