“That Thing” is used as a title for several Minecraft horror stories rather than one universally fixed character. In community discussions it often refers to an unidentified creature that is deliberately under-described: players notice impossible movement, a distant silhouette, corrupted terrain, or signs of intentional observation without receiving a clear explanation. Because the name is generic, different wikis and creators use it for different designs. This entry treats it as a category-level folklore entity rather than pretending there is a single authoritative biography.
