The Windows are anomalous architectural entities that resemble ordinary windows but may display impossible scenery, misleading reflections, or predatory figures. Different versions distinguish harmless-looking windows from active predatory manifestations. A wanderer may interpret a window as a view of the outside world, only to discover that the apparent landscape is inaccessible, false, or part of the entity’s trap. Their threat is therefore perceptual: the object looks like a safe architectural feature while quietly altering assumptions about space, exits, and what lies beyond the glass.
