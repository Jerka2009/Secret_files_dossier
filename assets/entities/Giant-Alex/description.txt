Giant Alex is a community-made horror legend describing an enormous version of Alex with a damaged or bloodied appearance. The classic story places the entity on a specially prepared OptiFine 1.12.2 setup using a specific seed. The creature is usually passive or observational rather than immediately aggressive: it watches from fog, forests, swamps, or distant terrain and leaves deep rectangular pits when it moves. Some retellings add crashes, pursuit, or direct attacks. Its recognizable visual hook is its scale — a familiar player model enlarged to roughly twelve blocks, making ordinary Minecraft geography feel suddenly miniature.
