SCP-966 consists of predatory creatures that resemble hairless, digitigrade humanoids. They are difficult to see under ordinary conditions and are primarily associated with the gradual disruption of human sleep. Victims can become increasingly sleep-deprived and vulnerable before the entities reveal themselves through specialized imaging or other indirect detection. The Foundation monitors reports of unexplained violent deaths associated with severe sleep deprivation as a way to locate surviving wild specimens. Their threat comes from combining stealth with an effect that weakens a target before any conventional physical attack occurs.
