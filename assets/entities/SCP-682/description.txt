SCP-682 is an intelligent, highly aggressive reptilian organism with exceptional durability and an apparent ability to adapt to damage and hostile conditions. The Foundation has repeatedly failed to destroy it permanently and instead uses heavy containment measures designed to keep it incapacitated. It can communicate, reason strategically, and respond to tests with deliberate hostility. Its biology changes under stress, making previous countermeasures unreliable. The combination of intelligence, regeneration, adaptive survival, and repeated containment breaches is the central reason the Foundation treats it as a Keter-level problem rather than as a conventional dangerous animal.
